Settings — appearance, refresh & developer options

Reached from the profile dropdown → Settings. Appearance switches between light, dark, or auto (device) theme. Schedule data force-refetches the latest calendar & class info, bypassing any cache. Developer settings is off by default; toggling it on reveals a field to point the app at a custom schedule URL, for testing alternate calendar sources without changing account data.
